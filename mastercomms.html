HOW TO USE THIS TOOL
Tap to expand — read before your first shift
▼
1
Find your situation. Tap a tab at the top to go to the right category — or use the search bar to find a message by keyword (e.g. "late", "quote", "weather").
2
Tap the card to expand it and read the message. Every card has a title and a situation description so you know exactly when to use it.
3
Tap "Copy Message" — the full text is now in your clipboard. Paste it into Jobber, iMessage, or wherever you're communicating with the client.
4
Fill in the brackets. Anywhere you see [INSERT NAME], [INSERT DATE], etc. — replace it with the real info before sending.
5
Keep messages consistent — add or edit a template when a new situation comes up. Saved changes are shared with the team.
